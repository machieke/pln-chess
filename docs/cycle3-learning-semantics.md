# Learning, dependence and uncertainty

Training supervision stays in `training/`; chess reference adjudication stays outside `src/pln_chess`. Production inference accepts observations, learned hypotheses and native runtime outputs only. The candidate grammar continues to seed piece geometry, castling edits, en passant edits and promotions; these are disclosed inductive biases, not learned discovery of every possible chess action.

An illegal contextual move updates its local legality conditionals. It does not discredit general geometry. Positive pseudo-legal or legal examples support movement projections. Separately supplied auxiliary labels update path occupancy, destination availability and boundary observations. These predicates are independently inspectable; their values do not authorize a hidden host conjunction.

The append-only ledger retains source, input, dependence group, transformation lineage, label and observation reliability. Duplicate payloads are idempotent; conflicting idempotency keys and corrupted hashes fail. Replay and rollback reproduce checkpoint versions. One group contributes at most once to a hypothesis. Labels from the same board/game or transformations share groups in the generated curricula; the system relies on honest supplied group/lineage identities rather than claiming automatic discovery of arbitrary statistical dependence.

For the contact curriculum, examples 0–3 per attacker type train attack relations, 4–5 train Attack→Exposure, and 6–7 train Exposure→NotLegal. The pools are disjoint by evidence and group. This removes the previous shared-training-evidence ambiguity from the unary exposure chain. Alternative local/exposure paths may still share evidence; they are selected or displayed as conflict, never revised together. The no-dependence ablation intentionally violates conservative training grouping for comparison and is not the recommended model.

Sensor reliability (FEN reading assumption 0.99), observation reliability (normally 0.5), learned rule TVs, inferred conclusion TVs, and empirical calibration are different quantities. PLN confidence is not the probability that a move label is correct. Calibration fits strength bins on the new validation split only and reports held-out Brier/ECE; it never feeds production classification. Small stratified samples make these calibration estimates exploratory.

Induction uses a finite empirical sampling frame: first observation per `(piece, dependence group)` in deterministic ledger order. A is a coarse local motif, B its first distance subgroup, C the legal-outcome class. N,A,B,C,BA,BC and evidence identities are recorded. Native Truth_w2c supplies confidence and native Truth_Induction produces the conditional TV. No evaluation label enters the population. The original 14 experiments are re-executed numerically.

This sampling frame is biased by ledger order and does not establish exchangeability of all chess positions. Induced hypotheses overlap their direct parents and are compared by native confidence choice. For valid input STVs, the induction confidence expression `Truth_w2c(sBC*cBC*cBA)` is at most 0.5, while mature direct rules can have greater confidence. The controlled induction on/off reports record every classification change, including a null result. Cross-piece induction and genuinely novel conjunctions are not claimed from these experiments.
