# Registered Cycle 10 study

Starting source: `257473d2462c75f84d3975bb91d85a8c1cc269dd`. The unchanged C8 checkpoint and pinned PLN/PeTTa runtime are the frozen baseline. Cycle 10 adds no native operator and does not authorize operational promotion. Local commits are permitted; pushes and deployments are excluded by the brief.

The [registration](../../configs/cycle10.json) fixes source collections, game and position quotas, opening-family partition, calibration bins, nested adaptation budgets and benchmark repetitions before any external inference result is inspected. Acquisition and sampling code is offline; production never imports the reference chess library.

The three collections are Lichess rated January 2013, Lichess official broadcasts January 2024, and TCEC Season 26 Premier Division at the pinned archive commit. They represent three game populations and two publishing organizations. Collection identity is not a claim of statistical independence of individual players, opening lines or engine opponents. Source bytes, publisher terms, licenses, timestamps, headers and original game identifiers are retained. Lichess downloads are checked against publisher SHA-256 lists. TCEC bytes are bound to the immutable Git commit and locally hashed; no independent publisher SHA-256 list was available. Source notices and ShareAlike terms accompany the derived corpus.

The first 1,000 valid complete standard records in each source form the sampling pool, or the entire file when smaller. A published result establishes a complete record, including resignation or adjudication; it does not imply the final board is a rules-terminal position. Invalid, short and unfinished records are counted and excluded before any model evaluation. Games are ordered by content hash. Global three-character ECO families (four-ply opening sequences when ECO is absent) are assigned by hash to adaptation, calibration or final test. Quotas per collection are 8, 8 and 34 games respectively, with 6, 6 and 10 positions per game. A game never crosses splits. Up to two special-move opportunities are sampled before positions spread across the record. This intentionally enriches special moves and is not a population-random sample.

Exact FEN, four-field canonical state, whole-board relative translation/color/file-reflection fingerprints, complete game content and opening-family overlap are audited. Historical datasets through Cycle 9 are included. Geometry-equivalent copies are conservatively excluded even when temporal metadata differs. Atomic local motifs intentionally recur: their transfer is the research question, not a leakage-free claim about every subrelation. Unseen structural combinations are reported separately from familiar local relations.

All 1,308 external positions and 112 additional synthetic adversarial positions are first evaluated using the unchanged C8 model, unchanged thresholds and FEN-only history policy. Strict and experimental modes are additionally checked on ten final-test positions per collection. Every new-cohort error or budget exhaustion is retained. The first evaluation does not use supplied event histories to hide FEN-only abstentions. Raw native strength/confidence, classifications, proof status, rule/evidence IDs and full native DAGs are retained.

After the frozen completion receipt, empirical calibration is fit on the separate external calibration games. The frozen Cycle 9 map and the new diagnostic map are evaluated on untouched final-test games. Calibration never feeds inference or changes classification. Ten strength bins and Laplace smoothing are fixed. Four hundred bootstrap draws resample complete games or controlled pairs, not independent candidate moves. Residual dependence within opening families, players and engine pairings remains a limitation.

Experimental adaptation uses nested subsets of 6 and 24 adaptation games. Up to 16 unique exact relative conditionals per game are selected using model coverage and deterministic hash ranking before reference labels are attached. Native Truth_Revision updates experimental checkpoint copies. The main dependence policy shares ancestry across games with the same opening family. Duplicate, transformed-ancestor, conflicting-observation, game-only and deliberately unsafe row-independent policies are compared. The final test cannot choose features, thresholds, sample budgets or model promotion.

Historical Cycle 9 adversarial and special-move abstentions are inspected individually. Supplied metadata is distinguished from supplied events and inferred historical relationships. Synthetic predecessor reconstructions are controlled interventions, never discoveries of actual missing history. Report unchanged original FEN-only results alongside these interventions. Evidence-removal and structural counterfactuals must accompany any claim of resolution. All positive predictions remain partial conditional evidence.

Timing runs are serial after other native study workers finish. Record cold/warm/reference proof equality, native calls, grounded atoms, RSS high-water marks, loading and replay overhead. Nine repetitions per new workload yield descriptive p50/p95/p99 statistics; sample maxima are not reliable tail guarantees. Historical timing is reproduced as a new measurement and compared with its original host-specific values.

Acceptance requires old C8/C9 proof parity, all 287 negative controls, unchanged runtime formulas, a clean bootstrap, automated tests, source/data integrity and browser verification. No research target overrides a failed mandatory gate. The package preserves failed experiments and historical evidence. Strategic work is an ontology and experiment proposal only; no playing score, potential-field computation or move-selection engine is enabled.

## Dependence prediction panel

Before external predictions, the secondary dependence panel is fixed to twelve final-test games: the first four game IDs per collection, one content-hash-selected position per game. All candidates are evaluated for the frozen model and seven dependence variants. This bounded diagnostic is not used to select a model or change thresholds. Transformed controls mirror ranks and exchange piece ownership, with explicit ancestor lineage; contradictory labels are declared synthetic supervision controls.
