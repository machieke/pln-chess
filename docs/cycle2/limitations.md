# Remaining limitations

- Legality is native-partial. Tier-4/5 relations are host-assembled local observation categories with learned native conditionals, not PLN conjunction proofs. Improved transfer does not establish full chess-rule learning.
- The seeded candidate grammar already encodes six piece geometric families. File symmetry, slider distance abstraction and occupancy-content projection are host inductive biases. No automatic relational program search or grammar induction is claimed.
- The exact native inheritance-induction wrapper has an argument-role mismatch for non-symmetric populations. B2 uses direct verified `Truth_Induction` with counted marginals and an explicit residual exchangeability assumption. Sampling first representatives of curriculum groups is biased; induction remains opt-in experimental.
- Positive lower-tier motifs mean compatibility in observed examples. Negative outcomes update complete local conditionals, not independent necessary constraints. Destination, obstruction and temporal effects are not universally composable.
- Exposure is learned and separately indexed; absence of evidence is not king safety. Native confidence choice is not a universal safety theorem. Conflicts abstain and can reduce recall. Full castling transit safety, unknown en-passant history, repetition and terminal-state reasoning are not fully generalized.
- The attacked-transit case `4kr2/8/8/8/8/8/8/4K2R w K - 0 1`, proposed `e1g1`, exposed a conflict-policy bug: the local conditional supports it, while exact observed counterevidence contradicts it. The final policy abstains on this conflict, covered by a regression test. This does not generalize a transit-safety theorem: post-move attack beliefs alone cannot establish transit safety, so C2-10 remains partial.
- Castling rights unknown and unknown occupancy abstain. FEN en-passant fields are taken as direct state observations; no full game-history reconstruction is attempted. `history_known=False` does not invalidate ordinary moves, and must not be interpreted as known repetition/draw history.
- The independent benchmark remains small and structured: 36 long-ray/clutter positions and four source games. Special-move generalization is chiefly historical/adversarial, not a large independent sample. The explicit irrelevant-context perturbation split has one pair.
- Baseline/ablation metrics can regress, especially with king moves and exposure. Exact-set accuracy and false-legal rates must be read with selective precision. Numeric targets are research goals, not a correctness guarantee.
- Warm memoization retains native outputs; it is not persistent PeTTa execution and has no eviction policy. Memory numbers are process high-water observations, not isolated aggregate native/runtime peak memory.

Next work: obtain a maintained native multi-premise rule with a dependence model; establish a statistically justified induction sampling frame; broaden independent special-move/game families and perturbations; learn more composable temporal and attack constraints; add cache eviction and repeated isolated benchmarks.
