# Learning and evidence attribution

The teacher supplies three kinds of observations: legal-move labels, positive auxiliary pseudo-legal movement labels, and individual successor attack labels. Only `training/cycle2.py` calls python-chess. The inference API receives a board and a checkpoint, never teacher labels.

A legal label updates the matched full local conditional and its distance/exact refinements. A negative legal label is not assigned to a general movement, destination or ray projection: the actual cause could be king exposure or another omitted condition. Positive legal or positive auxiliary movement examples support their applicable lower-tier motifs. Negative auxiliary pseudo-legal labels are retained in the dataset but do not indiscriminately discredit those projections. Thus lower tiers represent observed compatibility, not separately proved necessary conditions. Tier-4 negative obstruction/destination contrasts learn bounded joint local constraints and can generalize across distance and irrelevant board context.

Attack labels update separately scoped attack conditionals. Co-observed positive attacks support a learned Attack → WouldExposeOwnKing bridge, and their legal outcomes update WouldExposeOwnKing → Not(LegalMove). Both bridges start at the same finite uncertain prior as other hypotheses and are revised natively. Their evidence overlaps other hypotheses; they are conditional propagation paths, never independent revision inputs. No inference from absence of attackers asserts king safety.

The append-only ledger retains input FEN/move, source, dependence group, transformation lineage, targets, attribution explanations and payload hash. Hypothesis keys omit these provenance fields. The first record per hypothesis and dependence group contributes; later correlated records, including conflicts, are retained and listed as excluded. Shared projections are alternative views of the same evidence. Independent contradictions revise natively. Rollback replays a prefix, idempotency prevents repeated evidence, and model hashes cover schema-2 training metadata as well as beliefs.

Bulk ingestion validates all records before writing under one file lock and fsyncs the transaction. A crash can leave a valid prefix; retry is idempotent. It does not collapse observations into a count. Reproduction trains in a temporary ledger then replaces only generated Cycle 2 outputs; Cycle 1 originals and the baseline snapshot are never overwritten.

Induction is experimental and opt-in (B2). A deterministic sampling frame retains the first record per mover kind and dependence group. A is a reusable local descriptor, B its first observed distance subgroup, C the legal-outcome class. Counts N, A, B, C, B∩A and B∩C supply empirical frequencies. Confidence is obtained by native `Truth_w2c(B)` and the hypothesis by native `Truth_Induction`; neither uncertainty formula is reimplemented in Python. The residual exchangeability assumption is explicitly recorded. Sparse or degenerate marginals are excluded. These ordered curriculum representatives are not an unbiased sample of chess; B2 is an auditable experiment, not a calibrated population claim or automatic grammar discovery.
