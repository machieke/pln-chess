# Transfer protocol

`training/cycle2.py` deterministically generates seed 271828. Cycle 1 fixtures stay untouched. The Cycle 2 dataset contains training, validation, independent heldout-transfer, adversarial, controlled-translation and irrelevant-context partitions, with 40 independent held-out positions. Every synthetic starting board passes the reference validity check.

Training includes historical contrasts, short isolated rays and three source games. A recorded eligibility mask admits only slider distances 1–2 to learning, including source-game candidates; longer rays remain in the full evaluation sets. This prevents candidate enumeration from silently leaking the nominally held-out ray lengths. Validation uses medium rays with one extra opponent. Held-out uses longer rays (4–6), different blocker positions, two extra opponents, new king placements and four independent games at different depths. Adversarial examples include historical pins, check responses, mate/stalemate and additional clutter. Named families describe the generating structure, not merely FEN identity. Whole context families and source games are disjoint across train/validation/held-out; the overlap audit records shared local descriptors, which are the intended reusable knowledge.

Controlled translations and color flips share a declared training lineage and are reported separately. The explicit irrelevant-context pair adds a distant knight; its movement hypothesis ID and TV must remain unchanged, even though the original complete context fingerprint and opponent frames change. This is one focused perturbation pair, not a broad perturbation robustness claim.

All variants score identical complete bounded candidate sets, including invalid destinations. In-board metrics are reported separately. Expected legal move sets come from the evaluation oracle, not from the candidate grammar. Candidate-set completeness is measured in the tests and dataset audit; unknown and incomplete predictions count against recall. Transform tests compare only transformations preserving applicable state semantics.

The benchmark is synthetic research evidence, not a random sample of all chess. Forty held-out positions include 36 controlled structural families/variants and four game samples; confidence intervals are exploratory position-cluster bootstraps. More games, independently designed motif families, and repeated seeds are needed before generalizing the measured rates.
