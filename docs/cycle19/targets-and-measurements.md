# Targets and measurements

An episode binds one rook, one distinct occupied endpoint, two intervening cells, and one state identity. Clear means both intervening cells are empty. Attack means sliding influence through that path, including influence on a friendly occupied endpoint. This is not move legality, legal recapture, or king safety. NoAttack is the existing native obstruction contrary. No negative Clear rule is supplied; blocked Clear queries may therefore remain unknown.

`research/cycle19/measurements.py` implements two procedures. `clearance` derives the two intervening coordinates and audits occupancy directly. `influence` independently reconstructs a python-chess board and calls `Board.attacks`. Clearance never copies the influence label. Both procedures share an imported position and software environment; separate checks are not independent evidence. Each measurement records its implementation hash, input hash, method, source game/root/ancestry, role/state bindings and environmental parent. Annotations name their actual measured component and retain that lineage in parameter generations.

The three layers remain explicit: `reference` is the evaluator's fully specified state; `observed` is the raw information available to inference; `native.stages` contains derived support. For an E/O/U variant, E/O modify underlying occupancy, while U hides original occupancy from observations and retains it in the reference state. Thus a hidden blocker can be reference-negative while native inference returns unknown. Unknown does not become empty. Environmental corrections with incomplete reference states instead retain unresolved measurements.

Clear training requires an observed two-empty-cell antecedent. The terminal conditional requires a rook and a separately measured clear path, with an available known context. Each uses its own target label. False or unknown antecedents are exclusions rather than failed implications. Disagreement or unresolved measurement prevents admission. Supplied confidence .5 describes training trust; .99 describes environmental trust, and .95 the unlearned supplied templates. None is measured reliability.

The annotation payload's `admitted` flag records conditional eligibility. Actual admission additionally requires the registered training population and arm-specific A/B assignment; the model's `admitted_components` and native revision ledger are authoritative. Eligible development, test and transformed annotations never enter the primary fits. There are 48 Clear and 48 Attack training annotations, reused across the four fitted arms for 192 revision calls; these are not 192 independent observations.

The retained manual fixtures cover EE/OE/EO/OO, friendly destinations, reversed directions, and invalid state/role bindings. Their expected outcomes are declared by hand. A fault-injection test changes only the influence evaluator, confirms clearance remains unchanged, exposes disagreement and blocks admission. Fixtures and concrete episode measurements are under `artifacts/cycle19/measurement/`; model-specific admitted components are in `artifacts/cycle19/learning/models.json`.

The native adapter imports neither measurement nor probability code. Evaluation disables `Board.attacks` for the entire native query phase. Each Clear and Attack invocation receives the same raw typed facts and the complete frozen templates; no externally audited Clear is injected or transferred between invocations. Returned selections and retained statements are observable evidence; fired-rule parent trees remain unavailable.

On the 60 withholding variants, each of F, D-AB and S-AA returned unknown for all Clear queries. Each arm supported NoAttack in the 24 variants with a visible blocker. The other 36 Attack queries were unknown. On the 48 known-occupancy interventions, D-AB and F supported 12 positive Attack and 36 NoAttack. S-AA supported the 36 obstruction results and left the 12 open paths unknown. These counts describe repeated interventions on 12 games.

A separate read-only audit decoded all 2,512 content-addressed native stage proofs. Regenerating the inputs from each bound observed state and role pair reproduced the complete raw fact list. Comparing the declarations for its assigned model reproduced every rule, native truth value and stamp. Both invocations in every pair matched the registered budgets and runtime.
