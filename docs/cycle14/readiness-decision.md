# Readiness decision

**Continue preference research.** Cycle 14 does not justify a bounded move-selection experiment or a claim that a new native PLN capability is necessary. No move selection, new operator, legal checkpoint promotion, push or deployment is authorized by this decision.

The frozen immediate model answers 42 of 145 resolved directional references in the untouched 200-pair test: 24 correct and 18 wrong. Answered accuracy is 57.1% with a source-ancestry bootstrap 95% interval of 42.9–71.4%; directional coverage is 29.0% with an interval of 21.5–36.3%. The 80% accuracy and 50% coverage targets are unmet. There are fewer than 50 answered directions. Another three references are nondirectional and 52 remain unknown; predictions on those cases are retained but cannot establish directional agreement. These are new measurements from the [frozen evaluation](../../artifacts/cycle14/evaluation/summary.json), not a reinterpretation of the six answered Cycle 13 test cases.

On the same 50 temporal test roots, the two-ply model gets two of three answered directions correct among 33 directional references. The three-ply model gets four of seven correct among 28 directional references. Coverage is 9.1% and 25.0%, respectively. Accuracy intervals are 0–100% at two plies and 16.7–100% at three plies; 943 and 999 of 1,000 bootstrap replicates have defined accuracy. Twelve two-ply and 19 three-ply observations are incomplete because registered supplied lines end early. Against matched endpoint references, correct-answer yield is lower than the actual immediate-observation baseline by 3/33 and 1/28. Merely outperforming an immediate-only model that lacks endpoint descriptor keys is insufficient evidence of useful temporal transfer. See [the matched temporal study](temporal-study.md) and [ablations](ablations.md).

The TCEC-excluded preference fit answers four of 25 directional source-held-out references, with two correct and two wrong: 50% accuracy, 16% coverage and a 0–100% accuracy interval. This is a preference-training source exclusion. It reuses the common development-selected descriptor and frozen upstream checkpoints, so it is not a claim that every component of the pipeline is source-naive. The 60 source-held-out roots include one nondirectional and 34 unknown references. Operational deduplication and source-ancestry components do not prove universal statistical independence.

The registered reversal opportunities produce no demonstrated reference direction reversals and no successful model reversal patterns. The persistence fallback panel fails its intended structural criterion in all ten controls; most of its reference pairs are also unresolved. All ten opportunities and ten controls remain in the denominator at both horizons. The zero successful primary-horizon outcomes therefore establish neither a useful learned reversal capability nor impossibility for the native calculus. [Every failed case](preference-reversals.md) remains part of the evidence.

Concept preservation and uncertainty handling provide narrower positive results. Prior concept outputs are reproduced, and the new 80-group panel has 21 correct supported outcomes, 59 abstentions and no wrong supported classifications; 258 dependent controls pass. These controls are not 258 independent generalization cases. The unsafe dependence experiment inflates confidence in 135 rules while leaving the registered classification comparison unchanged. Dependence-aware evidence reuse, replay and rollback behave as specified. Those engineering results do not compensate for limited preference coverage. [Concept evidence](concept-generalization.md) and [dependence evidence](evidence-dependence.md) retain the relevant scopes.

The next study should prospectively register complete, reference-stable continuation pairs and structurally valid persistence controls. It should test whether relative descriptor collisions, contradictory evidence and incomplete observations account for the coverage failures, using disjoint source-ancestry groups and an explicit source-transfer design. Any refinement requires a new model generation and a new untouched evaluation cohort; the present final test remains frozen diagnostic evidence. Repeated searches and branch crossproducts must retain shared ancestry rather than increase the apparent independent sample size. A native capability question should follow a controlled demonstration that the existing conditional operators cannot express a required inference, not be inferred from this sparse reversal panel.

Engineering verification is assessed separately in the [twenty-package matrix](package-matrix.md), [engineering report](engineering-report.md), [performance measurements](performance.md) and [clean reproduction record](reproduction.md). Exact proof reconstruction and smaller summaries are auditability and interface outcomes; they do not establish strategic playing strength. The inspector requires the direct `/compact-preferences` route because its legacy homepage navigation link is absent. Dense response archives can exceed the interactive two-million occurrence limit; their offline audit uses a disclosed ten-million occurrence limit with unchanged encoding and exact evidence preservation. Complete historical CPU, interpreter-launch and transient-storage accounting is unavailable, so measured lower bounds must not be presented as verified global budget compliance.

The accepted Cycle 12 legal checkpoint remains the legal authority. Host-assembled relative observations, separate native conditional derivations, diagnostic calibration and offline reference comparisons retain distinct meanings. No supported joint strategic conclusion or global robust preference is inferred from their combination.
