# Independent concept transfer

Frozen field, concept and fork checkpoints answered **21/80** new independent structural cases correctly and abstained on 59. There were no wrong supported classifications, but positive recall including abstention was only **20/48 = 41.7%**, and overall answer coverage was **21/80 = 26.25%**. These limited conditional results do not establish complete strategic concept proofs. [Machine summary](../../artifacts/cycle14/concepts/summary.json) and [all native outputs](../../artifacts/cycle14/concepts/independent-holdout.json.xz) preserve every case.

Supported-only precision is 20/20; zero observed false positives among 32 negatives coexists with 31 negative abstentions. There are zero supported false negatives but 28 positive abstentions. Counting abstentions as successful negative classifications would be wrong. The 80 cases are independent under the registered game/raw-family checks; finite zero observed errors do not establish universal correctness.

All **258/258** registered transformations and premise-removal controls passed their explicit expectations. [Control outputs](../../artifacts/cycle14/concepts/holdout-controls.json.xz) retain the source lineage and native evidence. These controls check invariance or sensitivity of supplied bounded observations, not independent strategic transfer. No new concept model was fitted: C11 field, C12 concept and C13 role-fork checkpoints remain frozen. Their versions are recorded in the summary.

Every one of the 68 previous external abstentions was inspected in the [historical census](../../artifacts/cycle14/concepts/historical-abstention-census.json). The observed immediate cause was a missing exact learned conditional. Descriptor mismatch and insufficient independent support are related diagnostics; this study does not separately identify their causal effects. Query-scope loss and incomplete field evidence were not observed for those cases. The absence of native conjunction still limits joint strategic claims even where a single conditional is supported.

Fresh inference under the frozen Cycle 14 production source exactly preserved **466** historical native outputs: 370 historical test/calibration cases plus the 96-case external panel. The prior **306/306** test result and external **28 correct, 68 unknown, zero wrong** result are unchanged. The [preservation receipt](../../artifacts/cycle14/preservation/concepts.json) binds exact native proof parity and source hash. Previously exposed cohorts remain regression evidence.

The matched concept-enabled and concept-removed preference arms are reported in [the ablation study](ablations.md). A supplied concept schema is engineering knowledge; native revision of its conditional is learned knowledge. Neither a useful geometric relation nor a native conditional alone certifies legal recapture, tactical profitability or optimal play.
