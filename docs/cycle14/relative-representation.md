# Relative observations and bounded abstractions

The model target is a conditional preference between supplied A and B observations at a specified horizon. The board and move strings identify the environment; they do not become predictive features. [The ontology](../../src/pln_chess/cycle14/ontology.py) defines three registered alternatives: `c13-relational`, `role-change-v1`, and `shared-relation-v1`. Their choice is bounded before fitting and selected on development data only.

The C13 relational representation summarizes aggregate field changes. The role-change alternative additionally distinguishes the moving piece, its survival and owner-relative incoming/outgoing attack and defense changes. The shared-relation alternative adds bounded counts for relationships sharing an attacker or defender. Each observation uses categorical decrease/persistence/increase and explicit observed/partial/unqueried coverage. Optional concept channels are a separate matched arm. Horizon remains explicit; removing it or adding complete-position fingerprints would change the registered hypothesis class.

Piece identities bind relations within an observation but are canonicalized out of learned keys. Raw FEN, squares, UCI/SAN, source/game identifiers, evidence IDs and proof IDs remain provenance, not descriptors. Sorting the two candidate abstractions gives an order-independent conditional key with an explicit canonical A/B mapping. Reversing displayed order must reverse the directional output without manufacturing another independent observation. If both alternatives collapse to the same abstract observation, a directional label cannot train that key.

[Collision analysis](../../artifacts/cycle14/representation/census.json) compares independent training/development support, opposing labels, unknown content and descriptor collapse across the three bounded abstractions. The supplied categories are engineering assumptions; native truth-value revision learns the conditional channels within those categories. Finer categories can distinguish contexts yet reduce the number of independent observations per key. The development choice and final transfer results, rather than the mere existence of more keys, determine whether a refinement helped.

Seven executed [locality controls](../../artifacts/cycle14/representation/locality-summary.json) passed: piece-ID renaming, owner reversal, reflection, semantics-preserving translation, irrelevant-piece insertion, a distant blocker and an unknown ray cell. The longest checked sliding dependency spans seven squares. The implementation retains relevant occupied targets and the whole dependency path; no short geometric radius is imposed. These tests concern a local native relation and its mover-role projection. They do not claim that a whole-board aggregate should ignore every additional piece.

Unknown field content is never silently false, and missing mobility is never zero mobility. In the missing-context intervention, unavailable endpoint fields and roles remain explicitly unavailable. This often removes every directional contrast; such training rows are excluded as collapsed or incomplete rather than assigned convenient negative labels. The omission-robust arm is still trained on all eligible registered training-only missing-context examples, and its exclusions are a research result, not hidden data loss.

The observation packet is assembled by host code from separately derived native field and concept records. Its limited conditional interpretation and conservative observation confidence do not prove a native joint conjunction or fully propagate correlated premise uncertainty. Every output exposes this limitation, retains each native premise proof, and keeps legality independent from preference.
