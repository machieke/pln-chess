# Reversal and persistence experiments

The panel freezes ten structural reversal opportunities and ten persistence controls from the 50 new temporal test roots, before fitting or reading preference predictions. Selection reads native observation packets only, without reference labels. Complete attack/defense/mobility support changes prioritize reversal opportunities; unchanged support prioritizes persistence. Deterministic hash order resolves ties. If the structural criterion lacks ten eligible cases, explicitly marked fallback cases remain in the denominator. [Registration](../../artifacts/cycle14/datasets/reversal-registration.json) preserves every selected root and the observed support changes.

Three plies is the registered primary horizon; two plies is secondary. The same 20 independent root groups are evaluated at both horizons. A structural change is only an opportunity: it does not promise a reference reversal. Each case reports root and endpoint reference IDs, fixed actor, initial and continued native outputs, actual reference/model flip, disclosed target change, supporting ancestry and all failed conditions.

The [complete panel](../../artifacts/cycle14/reversals/panel.json.xz) includes three controls for every endpoint: omit the supplied context, remove the matching learned conditional, and refit after removing all supporting training ancestry. The latter removes the supporting games across all their horizons and descendants. It is an actual native refit, not removal of a displayed proof link. Unavailable or unchanged answers under these interventions remain visible.

“Answered reference pattern” means both native directions agree with resolved references and show the registered flip/persistence pattern. The stronger successful outcome additionally requires the registered structural criterion, native conditional proofs before and after, and changes to the endpoint answer under each removal control. Every failure, including unresolved references, initial/endpoint abstention or error, absent native proofs, unmet structural criteria and ineffective removal controls, is retained. Engineering execution passing does not mean reversal transfer succeeded.

Transfer here concerns source games and registered raw relative configurations excluded from training. Familiar abstract conditional keys are expected for generalization; their existence does not establish that every strategically relevant relation survived abstraction. A preference conditional on new supplied evidence is not a proof that the original candidate was objectively best, and a teacher-assisted line is not optimal-play evidence.

The [summary](../../artifacts/cycle14/reversals/summary.json) reports all ten opportunities and ten controls at each horizon, including successful and failed cases. The [engineering report](engineering-report.md) interprets the complete denominators. Successful illustrations, if any, are not presented as an unbiased population reversal rate.
