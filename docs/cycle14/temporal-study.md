# Supplied-context temporal study

The study distinguishes an **observation horizon** from the target evaluated by offline search. Two or three supplied plies describe a particular hypothetical path. They do not describe an exhaustive two- or three-ply strategic value or an optimal response. Reference scores at every endpoint retain the original decision-maker's perspective.

The [registered plans](../../artifacts/cycle14/datasets/temporal-plans.json) contain 240 roots: 180 new and 60 historical. The new test has 50 independent roots at each horizon, sharing the same 50 groups. Among these, 33 two-ply references are directional and 17 unknown; 28 three-ply references are directional and 22 unknown. Missing selected PV endpoints remain unavailable rather than being replaced by a branch selected for its reference or model outcome.

The new training subset has 80 roots; combined with 36 historical training roots it supplies 116 roots and 232 endpoint observations. The continuation models also receive 422 immediate training rows, for 654 registered training observations. These are not 654 independent games. Short branches and unknown labels remain in their registered population with explicit exclusions from native revision where required.

The [factorial results](../../artifacts/cycle14/temporal/summary.json) compare three fixed checkpoints—primary immediate, continuation-conditioned and omission-robust—using immediate observations, supplied endpoint observations and explicitly omitted context. All arms in a panel are scored against the **same endpoint reference labels**, on the same roots. The immediate-input arm changes its evaluation target to the endpoint while retaining root observations; its target-change count is reported. Comparing separate root-target and endpoint-target accuracies would not isolate temporal utility.

Separate paired contrasts measure temporal training with fixed immediate input, temporal training with the same supplied endpoint, added observations under the same checkpoint, context removal under the same checkpoint, and the omission-robust arm. Additional matched models vary the old/revised reference generation and old/selected descriptor. Horizon is part of the hypothesis grammar, so an immediate-only model may have no conditional for an unseen horizon. This architectural abstention is reported rather than mistaken for useful temporal reasoning.

Omission examples are constructed from training data only and use the same valid unknown-state representation as missing-context evaluation. Many may lose all directional contrast and be excluded by the frozen collapse policy. The training ledger reports that shortfall; no arbitrary direction is inserted to make the omission arm answer.

Each native endpoint conditional retains its field/concept observations, learned conditional, actor, horizon, ancestry and proof trace. Host comparisons disclose influence creation, removal and persistence without assigning a new temporal truth value. There is no native joint temporal theorem, joint-uncertainty propagation or robust preference over unexamined responses. [Reversal and persistence experiments](preference-reversals.md) and [branch coverage](response-coverage.md) test narrower claims. Final counts and paired estimates are summarized in the [engineering report](engineering-report.md).
