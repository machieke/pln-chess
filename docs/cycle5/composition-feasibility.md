# Positive composition feasibility

The pinned native library has executable modus ponens, negation, revision and confidence choice; inherited audits cover deduction, induction, inheritance wrappers, inversion and evidence stamps. The Cycle 5 extension runs literal compound, curried, contextual, intensional and variable-scope probes. [Exact expressions, inputs, outputs and source references](../../artifacts/cycle5/native-probes/audit.json) are paired with an executable MeTTa file.

Independent `Geometry` and `Destination` facts produce no conjunction or legal conclusion. A supplied `And` antecedent with a supplied implication derives `Local` at `(0.857, 0.5472000000000001)`. A supplied curried implication derives `Local` at `(0.8402599999999999, 0.3676571136000001)`. These are conditional applications of already supplied combined rules. Neither establishes that separate training discovered their conjunction. Currying/uncurrying and contextual/intensional candidates produce no demonstrated replacement theorem. Repeated-variable mismatch is a negative binding control, and shared-ancestor stamps reject revision.

The target knight capture `f6h5` receives separately learned geometry and destination beliefs. Geometry compatibility and evidence against destination conflict remain different propositions from global legality. Missing verified conjunction, path aggregation and universal king safety prevent a positive theorem. The strict planner therefore returns unknown with explicit proof gaps and preserves C4 local support separately. It never multiplies, minimizes or otherwise fuses premise TVs in Python.

Native unary negative chains are the strongest deployed alternative. They establish a reusable sufficient negative constraint using separately trained bridge evidence. Pooled raw local observations remain explicitly host-provided abstractions. No optional operator adapter or vendored patch is shipped. A new positive operator would require mathematical semantics, dependence rules, boundary controls and independently reproducible native execution before promotion.
