# Learning and evidence semantics

The new model replays the original eligible C4 training labels and a separately declared synthetic premise curriculum. Frozen C2–C4 test labels are never used for updates. A native `Truth_Revision` updates positive and negative conditional channels independently; confidence is not a probability and Python does not implement TV arithmetic.

At most one source/dependence/transform ancestry group contributes to each hypothesis. Repeated synthetic boards share their structural family group, so additional copies cannot increase confidence. Supporting and contradicting evidence lists remain available. New negative chains reject overlapping antecedent/bridge groups and decline a newly pooled rule with both label polarities. C4 evidence retains its original semantics and provenance.

The append-only ledger supports idempotent retries, rejects conflicting idempotency keys, verifies payload hashes and sequence continuity, and replays a prefix or retracts groups without deleting evidence. Schema 5 rejects corrupted checksums, noncanonical relation identities and incompatible runtime/schema imports. Earlier schemas remain loadable through adapters.

Counterfactual suppression removes the chosen typed family and affected inherited C4 local categories, then recomputes native results. Absence, negative evidence, low confidence and conflicting evidence are separate states. Remaining independent negative witnesses can keep a conclusion contradicted after one family is removed. All raw before/after TVs are retained.

Induction remains an isolated experiment. Neither a successful operator invocation nor a stronger local TV supplies a missing global legality proof.
