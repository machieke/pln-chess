Starting source: `f4a9fd5536d001fa8830773012dcd578443b0b61`.
The supplied Cycle 12 brief and `configs/cycle12.json` govern this cycle.
Registration precedes new inference implementation and new concept training.

The baseline runs in a fresh checkout and environment at the starting commit.
It reproduces the 463-test suite, 287 negative controls, 26 historical legal
suites, 48 Cycle 9 shards and 132 Cycle 10 shards, with complete native proof
parity against Cycle 11's current implementation evidence. It also regenerates
the Cycle 11 datasets and learned checkpoints, field evidence, transformations,

files are hashed and retained unchanged. Only orchestration, registration,
offline data construction and experiment preparation precede this baseline.

## Independent tracks

1. Evaluate the frozen Cycle 11 special-local candidate against the complete
   acceptance population. A positive decision requires every registered gate;
   failure remains visible and leaves the old legal model selected. New field
   and concept models are never eligible legal defaults.
2. Add typed field interactions and a dependency graph. Graph traversal schedules
   existing inference. It never manufactures a truth value or treats causally
   related observations as independent evidence.
3. Learn explicit local conditional hypotheses for eight concept families.
   Collect structural role observations separately from native field beliefs.
   Any compound observation packet supplied by the host is named as such in
   the premise and proof. Independent field conclusions are retained separately;
   their joint strategic interpretation remains partial where native composition
   is unavailable. Overload and trapping receive particularly conservative
   targets and explicit missing obligations or escape assessments.
4. Replay supplied sequences, including partial histories. Compute and retain
   every observed state, conditional field, concept candidate, dependency change,
   and evidence lineage. State differences are diagnostic comparisons, not
   native temporal implication rules. No sequence is presented as a plan.

## Data and evaluation commitments

Offline reference code may use python-chess. Production modules may not import
it or receive reference labels. Model selection, thresholds and native operator
scope are fixed before held-out inference. Transformations and counterfactuals
inherit ancestry. Canonical local role graphs, including equivalent sequences,
are audited across partitions; translation or ID changes cannot create an
independent observation. Raw-distance pooling and other supplied abstractions
are disclosed separately from learned evidence.

Report finite-sample precision, recall, false positives, false negatives,
abstention, transfer, proof completeness and evidence removal for each named
conditional target. These are not measures of a complete strategic theorem.
Holdouts that lack a matching conditional must remain unknown; failures must
not be fixed by moving examples between partitions or by supplying positive
rules. Empty models and selected-hypothesis removal are required controls.
At least five pin and five fork transfer attempts are retained regardless of
outcome, alongside at least ten independent held-out families.

The temporal panel includes at least ten supplied multi-move sequences covering
attack development, discovered attacks, defender removal, pins and release,
fork creation and escape, mobility restrictions, promotion and king exposure.
Every transition has independent offline labels and explicit source lineage.
Missing or conflicting history cannot be silently repaired into a complete
sequence. Every replay is compared with a repeat and with full recomputation.

Performance measurements follow correctness checks and run without competing
native regression workers. They cover realistic multi-piece positions, native
calls, cache reuse, wall time, memory, proof volume and exact full/incremental
TV, proof and evidence equivalence. Browser checks cover desktop and mobile,
selection and uncertainty, and exported proof inspection.

No new native operator, TV formula, legality gate, strategic score, move ranking,
or external publication is authorized. Cycle 13 recommendations will follow the
observed transfer and composition evidence, including unsuccessful experiments.
