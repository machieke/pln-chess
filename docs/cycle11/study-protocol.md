## Four field families

The primary representation is a typed relational graph. Its directional view indexes the same relations by owner-relative displacement. A board projection is a visualization only. Learned conditional keys contain piece-relative geometry, local occupancy and explicit relevant boundary/transition observations; they contain no absolute square or whole-board identifier.

Attack denotes geometric influence under observed occupancy and includes pinned attackers. Defense denotes geometric protection of a friendly occupied target; legal recapture is a separate unresolved or explicitly evaluated question. Mobility denotes ordinary occupancy-constrained movement potential, with operational legal-move classifications kept in their own channel. King exposure denotes individual opposing-source influence toward a king, supplemented by the accepted learner's native negative proof chains for candidate moves. Absence of a supported attack does not prove global safety.

All four families learn conditional truth values from positive and negative labels through the pinned native `Truth_Revision`. Query inference uses existing native modus ponens, negation and evidence selection. A directly observed joint local context may act as a single conditional premise; this is explicitly partial conditional inference, not a derived conjunction of independent uncertain predicates. Missing required occupancy yields unknown. Budget or runtime failures yield incomplete.

The generator and candidate grammar supply geometry categories, symmetry canonicalization and distance pooling; the labels supply the learned direction of influence. Report those contributions separately. Empty checkpoints and observation/model-evidence removal controls test that a deterministic geometry map is not being relabeled as a learned conclusion.

## Sampling, transfer and dependence

Controlled field-training queries cover the six piece classes, positive and negative directions, empty/friendly/opponent destinations, and empty/occupied rays. Sliding training distances are 1, 2 and 4; dedicated transfer queries use 3, 5, 6 and 7 where board semantics permit. Pawn/king/knight offsets retain their meaningful owner-relative geometry. Whole-position transformations and repeated observations retain the original ancestor and do not create independent training evidence.

Training, calibration and test generator groups are disjoint. External field panels use the pre-existing Cycle 10 game partitions and have no new acquisition or inferred independence across shared openings. Test selection is content-based and label-blind. Shared local descriptors are expected transfer, while whole-position and ancestry overlap is audited. Synthetic contexts establish controlled representational behavior, not a representative distribution of human games.

Measure precision, recall, abstention, native proof coverage, transformation invariance and counterfactual sensitivity separately for each field. Calibration measures concern the specified relation labels; raw PLN confidence is not an empirical probability. Use group bootstrap intervals where the population permits them, retain small-sample and dependence limitations, and do not fit thresholds on the test set.

At least ten before/after examples cover rook obstruction, bishop opening, knight relocation, pinned defense, discovered attack, king exposure, multiple defenders, unknown occupancy, promotion and en passant. At least twenty controls cover valid translation, file reflection, color-relative orientation, ID renaming, unrelated distant changes and relevant blocker changes. At least five transfers must involve configurations withheld from field training, with exact descriptors and pooling assumptions disclosed.

## Temporal integrity and incremental reuse

A new append-only claim transport records delivery order separately from claimed event order. Duplicates do not add independent evidence; contradictory claims remain visible; gaps and conflicting provenance prevent silent completion. Structural replay does not validate chess legality. Direct observations, trusted metadata, inferred structural consequences and unknown history remain distinct. The original Cycle 10 receipt contract remains reproducible.

Incremental field reuse requires an exact match of the model, runtime, inference options and complete relevant dependency signature. Signatures include source/target identity and kind, every relevant ray cell, occupancy completeness and any required boundary state. Promotion, ordinary capture, en passant and castling effects invalidate all affected relations. An unrelated distant change may preserve a local proof while the enclosing snapshot binding changes. Full recomputation must return identical relation values and proof IDs.

Benchmark cold full fields, warm fields, incremental updates, individual and multiple candidates, cache hits/invalidations, native calls/atoms, proof sizes and memory. Nine repetitions per registered workload are descriptive; small-sample tail estimates and host drift are reported. Timed native runs do not overlap other study inference workers.
