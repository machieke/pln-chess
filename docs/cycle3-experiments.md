# Cycle 3 experiment protocol

The frozen Cycle 2 dataset and checkpoints remain byte-preserved. `baseline.json` records exact split IDs, source artifacts, dataset/runtime hashes, thresholds, machine information and candidate-level reproduction. The frozen split has 40 positions, 2,635 candidates and 851 legal moves. The old adversarial split has 24 positions. Cycle 3 is informed by analysis of Cycle 2 errors; improved results on that old split are retrospective engineering regression results, not an untouched test of a newly selected research hypothesis.

The new curriculum uses seed 314159. It retains only the eligible short-ray Cycle 2 training examples and adds 48 independent isolated contact positions, eight per attacker kind. The mask still withholds long slider movement examples from the old training component. The added contact curriculum contains only the moving king and opponent contacts; it supplies attack knowledge rather than long moving-slider labels.

New evaluation includes 23 combined-condition adversaries (pins of five piece types, orthogonal/diagonal discoveries, double check, checking-piece captures and blocks, king captures/escape, castling phases, en passant ray opening, and promotions), eight independently seeded games, and four separate validation games. Color flips and valid horizontal reflections are separately labeled paired splits; they share test-family lineage and must not be treated as independent families. FEN validity, legal-move candidate coverage, train/test FEN and family overlap, exact generation seed, dataset hash and family support are checked and recorded. Relative primitive overlap is intentional; whole training/test families are distinct.

All reported scores enumerate full bounded candidate sets. In-board-only, per-piece and per-family breakdowns prevent out-of-board negatives from masking failures. False positives, self-check errors, abstention, exact move sets, inference latency, native calls and grounded atoms are recorded. Precision/recall intervals use 300 deterministic position-cluster bootstrap draws; family dependence and small sample sizes remain limitations.

The comparison includes B0, B1/B2, C3-local, C3-exposure, C3-induction, C3-full, and no-obstruction/no-exposure/no-induction/no-arbitration/no-dependence/exact-context-only variants. Controlled inference variants share an identical trained parent and evidence ledger; only the named feature changes. Representation/dependence ablations are retrained from the same training rows. `C3-local` also contains the new contact training evidence in its local conditionals, so its comparison with frozen B1 does not isolate exposure alone; the same-parent exposure ablation does.

The no-arbitration variant disables conservative conflict abstention and leaves native confidence choice alone. It is an experimental comparator, not a stronger safety claim. Exact-context-only is a deliberately narrow memorization diagnostic. All variants retain native execution; B0's original implementation has no Cycle 3 scalar-cache acceleration.

`artifacts/cycle3/comparison.json` indexes compressed per-candidate reports. Each row records FEN, split/family, candidate, oracle label, predicted class, raw TV, matched hypotheses/rules, proof identity, evidence IDs and abstention reason. Full error proofs are retained, and any candidate can be reconstructed with `pln-chess inspect`. `calibration.json`, `learning-curves.json`, `induction.json`, `benchmark.json` and `self-check-resolution.json` contain the complementary studies. Sparse learning curves use the first 1, 2, 4 and 8 independent training groups in fixed ledger order, not a test-selected curriculum.
