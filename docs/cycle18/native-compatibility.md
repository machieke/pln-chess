Source mapping closes the observation-identity and annotation-multiplicity gap.
It does not supply a population sampling model, noise law, joint dependence law
or confidence interpretation. Native strict disjointness therefore remains the
conservative default. The bounded disjoint arrangement remains usable under its
demonstrated native conditions, without being a universal independence solution.
