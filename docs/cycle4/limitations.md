# Limits and interpretation

* Supported-positive classifications use an explicit joint local projection. There is no complete native movement AND path AND destination AND king-safety theorem. Native unary chains provide stronger negative conclusions, not universal positive safety.
* Candidate offsets, role projections, structural successor edits, and the choice of abstract targets are supplied inductive bias. They are not learned chess laws. Candidate coverage is measured against the reference engine separately.
* Unknown history remains unknown. A supplied history record verifies structural consistency and an attestation's scope, not the legality of every historical move. Bare FEN en-passant and castling flags do not prove their full histories.
* Evidence independence is declared by provenance groups. The conservative first-contribution rule can discard useful correlated observations. Source retraction and deterministic rollback are supported; automatic statistical dependence estimation is not.
* The induction control isolates activation and redundancy. It does not demonstrate a production gain over the same explicitly supplied abstraction. Production induction remains disabled.
* Frozen Cycle 2/3 tests are retrospective regressions after earlier error analysis. New Cycle 4 games use predeclared seeds, but 12 games are a small, correlated sample of random play. Bootstrap intervals are exploratory. Translation controls intentionally share training lineage and do not count as independent generalization.
* Host proof construction still repeats logical occurrences, even though pure native calculations are cached. A proof-byte estimate is not an operating-system RSS limit. Peak process RSS includes whichever research data the process has loaded; it must not be described as inference-only memory.
* Native exception or budget exhaustion yields incomplete results. A timeout does not justify a guessed classification. No strategic evaluation or move selection is provided.
