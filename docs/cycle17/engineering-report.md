# Cycle 17 engineering report

Both registered disjoint-training models compose learned two-cell clearance with
a learned rook attack rule through the unchanged native reasoner. Both shared
models derive clearance but leave attack unknown. One shared training source is
enough to reject the tested matching pair. This is a bounded native composition
result, not a solution to correlated uncertainty, generic recursion or chess
move selection.

The architecture decision is **A + C**: retain the demonstrated disjoint route
under the current guard, and recognize that shared joint uncertainty is not
identified by the existing truth values and flat stamps. The isolated exact
reference models specify the missing assumptions; they never supply chess
answers, checkpoint parameters or cached predictions. No replacement evidence
policy is implemented.

The exact laboratory demonstrates why equal marginals do not determine joint
success: two fair binary parameters can yield 0, 1/4 or 1/2 under different joint
laws. A shared two-point parameter gives 5/16 for two conditionally independent
applications, versus 1/4 for independently redrawn parameters and 1/2 for repeating
the same event. Conditioning once and reusing a posterior differs from counting
one likelihood twice. Diamonds, cycles, unequal marginals, a false hypothesis and
missing dependence retain explicit assumptions and bounds. See
[reference semantics](reference-semantics.md) and the
[future policy requirements](evidence-policy-decision.md).
