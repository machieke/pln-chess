## Source and label protocol

The external tactical pool is a frozen prefix of 20,000 rows of the [Lichess puzzle database](https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles), acquired before preference modeling. The source is CC0. Its FEN precedes the opponent's first listed move; apply that move before constructing the puzzle comparison. One selected puzzle per source game prevents repeated positions from becoming independent evidence.

The independent-game pool uses the existing 150 published games with their original source, game IDs, histories and collection attribution. Select at most one primary pair per game, using a deterministic position policy. Old legality labels never become preference labels. Keep external-game, external-tactical and controlled demonstration cohorts distinguishable.

Offline labels use the pinned [Stockfish 18 release](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/releases/tag/sf_18), with recorded source/archive/network/binary hashes, one thread, fixed hash memory and fixed node budgets. Retain raw engine output, searched continuations and both tested candidate roots. Engine scores appear only in label evidence and evaluation diagnostics. Production descriptors and model rules cannot access them.

Use two separately reset searches of 20,000 and 80,000 nodes per pair, with MultiPV over the two explicit candidate roots. A directional label requires the same ordering at both budgets and a margin of at least 80 centipawns at each, or consistent mate ordering. Two non-mate margins at most 20 centipawns yield `no-demonstrated-preference`. Budget disagreement, intermediate margins, missing PVs and unresolved score bounds yield `unknown`. These are disclosed supervision rules, not strategic utilities or calibrated probabilities. Puzzle solution annotations remain additional independent-source provenance, not a guarantee that the local engine agrees.

Candidate roots must be selected before looking at preference scores. Puzzle pairs compare the supplied solution with a deterministically selected legal alternative. Game pairs compare the published move with a deterministically selected legal alternative. Alternate displayed A/B order using a fixed hash, so dataset order does not reveal the label. The offline chess library may verify labels and examples; it cannot decide production legality.

Aim for at least 300 independent source-game pairs and 100 independent structural groups. Include attack creation, defense preservation/removal, mobility, exposure, pins, forks, discoveries, promotion, threat removal, quiet moves and ambiguity; record actual coverage without relabeling examples to meet targets.

Retain a held-out source/distribution-shift panel separately. Transformation controls inherit their parent's split and dependence group. No transformed copy increases effective sample size. Bootstrap evaluation at the connected-component level, with a fixed seed.

## Descriptors, inference and uncertainty

Every candidate supplies a structurally reconstructed successor and an independent legality belief. Field snapshots have an explicit bounded entity/query scope shared between the two candidates. A missing field is unobserved, not negative. Field counts and transitions are diagnostic categorical features, with separate unknown and incomplete counts; they are never weighted into a strategic score.

Registered abstractions include attack-only, defense-only, mobility-only, exposure-only, relational changes without concepts, immediate consequences without continuations, weaker pooled relations, and the full categorical descriptor. Report all variants, including low coverage. Do not select a winner using held-out labels. The primary immediate comparison uses relative field-change categories; a separate temporal model includes explicitly supplied continuation consequences. Canonicalizing the pair by relative descriptors makes reversed queries share the same evidence. Identical descriptors cannot justify a directional preference.

Use independent native channels for canonical-first, canonical-second and no-demonstrated preference. Unknown labels are retained but do not become false evidence. Duplicate evidence is idempotent; ancestry components with internally contradictory labels are withheld and inspectable. Independent opposing observations remain in the ledger and are revised natively. A and B are never assumed transitive. Contradictory supported directions produce an indeterminate result. Confidence is not probability of correctness.

Each result reports raw strength/confidence, model/runtime identities, the descriptor, rule/evidence/dependence IDs, native expressions, proof graph, observation provenance and unresolved relationships. `incomplete` is distinct from `indeterminate`; budgets and failures cannot silently return complete predictions. No preferences are promoted into operational move selection.
