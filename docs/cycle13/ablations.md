All eight registered descriptor variants use the same partitions and label policy. Relational and without-concepts are intentionally identical four-field arms; full adds bounded concept consequences. Attack-only, defense-only, mobility-only, exposure-only and weaker-relative remove or coarsen designated categorical observations. No held-out result selects a replacement primary model.

The dependence intervention repeats 222 training records three times under distinct delivery identities. With grouping, native truth values remain identical to the original fit. Without grouping, confidence increases for 44 of 44 rules despite zero additional independent observations. Duplicate appends are byte-identical, internally conflicting dependent components are withheld, and independent opposing labels remain inspectable in 8 primary rules. The reported prediction contradiction rate counts simultaneously supported opposing channels; zero such predictions would not mean that training evidence is unanimous.

340/340 corpus reversals preserve rule/evidence identity and swap directional channels. Removing the query conditional yields abstention for all 56 held-out removal controls, including already absent rules. A separate controlled three-edge native cycle demonstrates nontransitivity; removing its A/C evidence leaves A/C indeterminate. Those invented categorical assumptions are not empirical chess examples.

Correlated descriptor improvements do not establish causal strategic importance. The dependence intervention does demonstrate confidence inflation caused by repeated counting in that controlled replay. Temporal observation/training changes are documented separately.
