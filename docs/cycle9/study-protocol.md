# Registered Cycle 9 study

The [registration](../../configs/cycle9.json) was written before inspecting final held-out predictions. The accepted checkpoint is unchanged. The development experiment uses only the 20 development games; calibration uses 20 different games from two other opening families; the final game test uses 100 games from ten other families. There is no Cycle 9 operational training, threshold search, or repeated tuning on the final game test.

The [dataset manifest](../../artifacts/cycle9/datasets/manifest.json) identifies 140 complete synthetic games, 700 sampled nonterminal game positions, 128 additional adversarial positions and 80 dedicated special-move positions. Games run to automatic reference termination, including fivefold repetition and 75-move draws. A ply cap is not counted as a completed game. Five positions are sampled across each game's duration after excluding historical and cross-split equivalent boards. Seeds, full move sequences, initial/final states, termination reasons and sample plies are retained in the [dataset](../../artifacts/cycle9/datasets/dataset.json).

These are seeded random legal games after named opening prefixes, not human games or strong-engine play. Structural families and game source identifiers are separate fields. The leakage audit excludes full-board translated, reflected and color-transformed equivalents against previous datasets and between current positions. It deliberately permits transferable local subrelations to recur. Games within an opening family are not assumed to be independently sampled human chess.

The adversarial cohort comprises 64 controlled pairs. Pair members share all distractors and differ in a registered blocker, occupant, attacker, castling-transit or en-passant-discovery condition. Their expected behavior is either sensitivity to a legality-changing edit or invariance under an irrelevant edit. Each pair is one dependence group. Promotion choices, captures, king exposure and boundary interactions are additionally exercised by the special-move cohort and existing regressions. Independent labels come from the offline reference; they are never passed into native inference.

Operational inference scores every candidate in all 908 positions. Strict and experimental comparisons each score all 80 special-move positions and the first registered 25 independent-game positions. Their limited independent-game sample is stated explicitly; it is not represented as a 500-position strict-mode study. All established Cycle 8 operational suites, plus all previously reported strict and experimental comparison suites, are replayed separately.

Reports separate complete candidate sets from in-board candidates. Exact-set accuracy compares the predicted legal set with every reference legal move. The generator checks that the bounded candidate grammar contains the entire reference set for every sampled position; any missing move fails dataset construction. Off-board negatives therefore cannot hide a candidate-generation omission or inflate the reported in-board precision.

Calibration fits ten strength bins using `(legal + 1)/(count + 2)` on calibration games only. Empty bins return one half and retain a zero sample count. Separate mappings are fit for complete and in-board populations. Raw strength, raw confidence as a diagnostic ranking score, a strength/confidence diagnostic combination, and the empirical mapping are evaluated separately. The calculations are offline statistical diagnostics, not replacements for native truth-value arithmetic. No estimate is fed into classification or inference.

Reliability tables, Brier score, clipped log loss, expected calibration error, confidence-stratified precision/recall, selective risk/coverage and piece/special-move/evidence-volume strata are exported. Wilson bin intervals are explicitly marked as ignoring dependence. Overall metric intervals resample source games or controlled pairs with a fixed seed. A zero-error bootstrap interval is not a guarantee of zero risk; opening-family dependence and synthetic sampling remain limitations.

Performance comparisons use identical fixed fixtures and seven serial repetitions per reference, cold and warm arm. Full candidate payload digests, including native proof traces, must match within and across implementations. Six workloads cover a single promotion candidate, a sparse position, the historical 214-candidate full position, promotion-heavy, adversarial and independent-game positions. Measurements retain native calls, grounded atoms, cache hits, truncation, parent and child memory high-water marks, checkpoint size and loading time. Seven samples cannot estimate reliable population p95/p99 tails.

The composition track reviews the existing specification and adds exact mathematical counterexamples. It performs no new native composition search, defines no production operator and changes no native calculus. Its recommendation is independent of operational benchmark outcomes.
